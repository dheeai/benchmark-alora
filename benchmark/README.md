Video is 25 fps, five seconds, H.264 up to 720p without upscaling; audio is embedded AAC and a paired mono 48-kHz PCM16 WAV. Source audio start timestamps are accounted for during video extraction. Frame resampling may repeat up to two ending frames to complete the five-second grid.

Selection required at least 80% automated speech coverage, less than 0.1% clipped samples, and no detected scene cut at threshold 0.25. Sampled frames were reviewed separately; this is not exhaustive playback. Every retained MP4 decodes to 125 frames, every WAV has 240000 samples, and paired audio passed waveform comparison. Manual lip synchronization, speech quality, language, transcripts, demographics and generation permissions remain unverified. All generation_training_ready flags are false.

Discovery covered 132 distinct search candidates across 12 queries; 32 source descriptions and licenses were checked. Seven candidates were excluded before download for missing reuse evidence, unclear provenance or insufficient language context, and recordings already represented in the dataset were excluded from this release. Search relevance and scene appearance do not certify language or demographics. See validation-report.json for all extraction outcomes.
